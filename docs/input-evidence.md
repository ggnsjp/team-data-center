# Evidence for the inputs: the model's values against published ones

For the inputs marked as assumptions: the range found in published sources, where the model's value sits in it, and how far the results move when the value moves to the end of the range. Every source was opened and checked between 2026-11-26 and 2026-11-28. The list of all values is in [data-sources.md](data-sources.md).

This page is written from `src/dcopt/common/evidence.py` (`uv run python -m dcopt.common.evidence --write`). [日本語](input-evidence.ja.md)

## What moves the result most (theme 1)

1. **The assumed sites carry most of the result.** Of the heat the published plan delivers (net benefit 93.8 M yen a year), 49 % goes to two assumed sites, a fish farm and a road-heating area. Without any assumed site the best plan found is worth 11.4 M yen a year (13 sites, 6.0 km). The fish farm and the road heating need only 22 to 25 C and take waste heat without a heat pump; existing buildings need 45 to 60 C and a heat pump each.
2. **The size of the demands comes next.** With every yearly demand at 0.7 times the net benefit is 28.4-51.5; at 1.3 times, 144.8. The demand per building is an assumption that does not use floor area; published intensities confirm the order of magnitude and no more.
3. **Costs are in 2019 prices.** With pipe costs at fiscal 2025 prices (1.22 times) the net benefit is 72.7-76.6; at a 4 % discount rate 78.3-79.0; with a pipe life of 40 years 108.4.
4. **The kerosene replaced and its price.** Between 7.6 and 8.7 kWh of heat per litre the net benefit is 115.0 to 80.7; with 135 yen/L in the low-price scenario 112.2; with electricity at 30 to 34 yen/kWh 87.4 to 82.4.
5. **The amount of waste heat and the heat loss matter least.** Waste heat is in surplus: a recovery share of 0.33 gives 84.3, half the data-center load 80.4, and a heat loss of 3.7 to 4.5 % instead of 2 % gives 92.0.

The numbers come from `experiments/input_sensitivity/run.py` and `experiments/heat_loss_check/run.py`: one input moved at a time; a range "a-b" spans the published pipes kept and the plan made again.
